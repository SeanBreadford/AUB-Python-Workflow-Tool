    Welcome to the Project Browser

    This shows where every shot and asset is up to. It mostly keeps itself up to date: when you save a file into the project, your task moves to In progress on its own.

    It's shown next to notes and changes you make, and used for My Tasks and your Inbox. Use the same name your team assigns you by.

    How it works

    The grid

    One row per shot or asset, one column per task (layout, 3D, comp…). Each chip is that task's status. Click a chip to see its versions and notes, and to update it.

    Rings on a chip

    • Approved ! Something's wrong. For example, a new version was saved after approval, or two people saved the same version. Open it to see what to do.
    • In progress … Gone quiet. Nothing new for a while. Worth checking in.

    What happens by itself

    • Saving the first file for a task moves it to In progress.
    • Saving a new version after a Retake moves it back to In progress.
    • Approving or submitting remembers which version it was, so newer ones get noticed.

    Finding your work

    • My Tasks shows only what's assigned to you.
    • Inbox counts notes and changes on your tasks you haven't seen yet.
    • Activity lists everything that happened, newest first.
    • Press / to search, Esc to close a panel.
